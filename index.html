Privacy Policy

Dusk

A simple Discord bot built to execute commands without collecting or storing personal information.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Dusk does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The bot simply processes the information required by Discord to receive and execute commands.

Information We Collect

Dusk does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information from its users. No user profiles, personal databases, or tracking systems are maintained by the bot.

How the Bot Works

When you use a Dusk command, the bot may temporarily receive information provided through Discord's API that is necessary to process and respond to that command. This information is used only for the immediate operation of the requested feature.

Data Storage

Dusk does not intentionally store user data, message content, Discord user information, or server information in a permanent database.

Data Sharing

Dusk does not sell, rent, trade, or share user information with third parties. The bot does not operate an advertising or tracking system.

Third-Party Services

Dusk operates through Discord and is therefore subject to Discord's own systems and policies. Information handled directly by Discord is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Dusk changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Dusk's handling of information, you can contact the bot's owner or administrator through the official Discord support channel.
